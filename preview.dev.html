Connect Canvas

Easel reads your assignments and classes straight from your school's Canvas.

You log in on your school's own Canvas page, so Easel never sees your password and stores nothing secret. If you log out of Canvas, log in again here.

Use an access token instead

A token keeps Easel connected even when you log out of Canvas. It is stored only on this computer, in this browser's extension storage, and is never sent anywhere except your Canvas server. You can revoke it at any time from Canvas settings.
